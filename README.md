## 🔬 Research Focus

My research examines the **processing–structure–property–performance relationships** governing metallic materials produced by **laser powder bed fusion (LPBF)**, with particular emphasis on **duplex and super duplex stainless steels**.

I investigate how rapid solidification, repeated thermal cycling, alloy chemistry, and post-processing affect:

* ferrite–austenite phase balance and phase stability;
* crystallographic texture and grain structure;
* residual stress and local lattice distortion;
* microstructural heterogeneity and defect populations;
* mechanical response and deformation behaviour; and
* electrochemical response and corrosion performance.

```text
LPBF Processing + Alloy Chemistry
               ↓
Rapid Solidification · Thermal Cycling
               ↓
Microstructure · Phase Balance · Texture
Residual Stress · Defects
               ↓
Mechanical Response · Electrochemical Response
               ↓
Material Performance
```

My broader objective is to establish physically defensible links between **non-equilibrium LPBF metallurgy**, subsequent microstructural evolution, and the resulting engineering performance.

## ⚙️ Computational Modelling

I use computational methods to complement experimental characterization and to examine material response beyond what can be directly resolved experimentally.

Current modelling interests include:

* finite-element analysis of metallic deformation;
* **J2 elastoplastic constitutive modelling**;
* stress, strain, and plastic-strain field evolution;
* experimental–numerical comparison;
* phase-field and microstructure-evolution workflows;
* parameter sensitivity and numerical verification; and
* provenance-controlled and reproducible post-processing.

Where modelling is used, I distinguish between **numerical verification**, **parameterized reduced-model descriptions**, and **physically validated predictive models**.

## 🧭 Research Approach

I aim to keep experimental interpretation and computational modelling traceable to measurable evidence.

```text
Experiment
   ↓
Quantitative Characterization
   ↓
Data Analysis
   ↓
Mechanistic Interpretation
   ↕
Computational Modelling
   ↓
Verification · Validation · Reproducibility
```

Particular attention is given to **uncertainty, competing interpretations, numerical verification, data provenance, and reproducibility**.
