### 🔬 Research

I investigate **processing–structure–property–performance relationships** in metallic materials, specializing in **laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) of duplex and super duplex stainless steels**.

My research integrates **microstructural characterization, mechanical testing, electrochemistry, and computational modelling** to examine how alloy chemistry, processing, and thermal history influence material performance.
